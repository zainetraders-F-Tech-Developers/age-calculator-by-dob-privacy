Privacy Policy — Age Calculator By DOB

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Age Calculator By DOB ("the app") is developed by F-Tech Developers. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Information the app collects

The app itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All calculations (age, age difference, days between dates, date calculations, birthday countdowns, cut-off age checks, chronological age, and leap year checks) are performed entirely on your device. Any birthdays you save inside the app are stored only in the app's local storage on your device and are never sent to us or to any server.

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, location, or any other personal data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and share the following data, as described in Google's own policies:

  • Approximate location
  • App interactions
  • Diagnostics
  • Device or other IDs (including the advertising ID)

This data is encrypted in transit. It is used for advertising/marketing purposes, and app interactions and diagnostics may also be used for analytics. You can learn more about how Google uses this data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only sharing is the AdMob data described above, handled by Google.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
